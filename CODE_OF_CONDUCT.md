# Code of conduct

Fhemni welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and
level of experience.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good faith, and give
specific technical feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, deliberate misinformation, and disruption are not acceptable.

Political disagreement is expected in a project about public-affairs media. It
must remain evidence-focused and may not become campaigning, personal attacks,
or pressure to make Fhemni endorse a party or candidate.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the project and its community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the project owner through GitHub rather than posting sensitive
details publicly.
